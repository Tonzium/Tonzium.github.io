## What I Do

I build AI and data systems that turn raw real-world data into decisions and measurable value. Senior Process Engineer at Murata by day, M.Sc. from Aalto University, with a deep and growing focus on machine learning, data engineering, and applied AI. All of it proven in live industrial production, not just notebooks.

!!! success "Highlights"

    *   **AI in production:** predictive maintenance pipelines and deep learning defect classification running on real process data.
